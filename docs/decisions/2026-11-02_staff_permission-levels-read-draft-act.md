- [staff] **Decision (2026-11-02): every Odd holds one permission level (read, draft or act), only the owner raises it, on the Odd's Settings tab or with one yes to the Odd's own ask for it (`ask_for_level`), and a level says how much an Odd does on its own, never what it may reach: act covers housekeeping inside the office plus the tools the owner connected for that Odd at write, and at draft a write grant works too, with each call that changes something waiting on the owner's desk as that exact call, except a tool the owner lets that Odd use without asking.** Anything else outward is a draft that waits for the owner's yes on that exact action; giving an Odd both act and a write grant is that yes for the tool, as hard rule 4 says, and Odds are told so in every run. At draft the gateway passes reads on and holds each change as a `call` ask showing the tool and its exact inputs; on the owner's yes the office makes that call itself, once, and the Odd carries on with what it returned in a new run. Read never proposes a change: a write grant waits until the Odd is at draft or higher.
